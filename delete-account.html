Delete Your Account

Last updated July 27, 2026  ·  Applies to OFFUNIT accounts

Delete from inside the app

The fastest way to delete your OFFUNIT account is directly in the app. Deletion is immediate and permanent — it does not require contacting us or waiting for approval.

  1. Open OFFUNIT and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap Settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  4. Tap Delete Forever, then Yes, Delete Everything on the final confirmation.

Deleting your account cannot be undone. You will not be able to recover your profile, matches, or conversations afterward.

Delete by email

If you can no longer access the app — for example you uninstalled it, lost access to your device, or cannot sign in — email us and we will delete your account for you.

  • Email: contact@offunit.app
  • Subject line: Account deletion request
  • Send from the email address on your OFFUNIT account, so we can verify the request

We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days, and in most cases much sooner.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes the following from the platform:

  • Your profile, including your name, age, bio, prompts, and professional details
  • Your photos and any featured GIF
  • Your quiz answers and personality result
  • Your matches, conversations, and messages
  • Your OffFeed posts, comments, and reactions
  • Your badges, including verified status
  • Your nursing license details, if you submitted them. We never store your license photo, so there is no copy of it to delete
  • Your location, matching preferences, and notification settings

What we may keep, and why

A limited amount of data may be retained after deletion, only where it is reasonably necessary:

  • Safety and moderation records. If enforcement action was taken on your account, we keep a record of the action and an evidence snapshot, for the safety of the community, dispute resolution, and legal compliance.
  • Records we are legally required to keep, such as transaction records.
  • Backups, which roll off automatically on a fixed schedule.

This is described in full in Section 9 of our Privacy Policy.

Purchases, subscriptions, and balances

Deleting your OFFUNIT account does not automatically cancel a subscription. Subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google, and must be cancelled in your store account.

  • iPhone / iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → OFFUNIT → Cancel Subscription
  • Android: Google Play → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → OFFUNIT → Cancel

Cancel your subscription before deleting your account, so you are not billed for a renewal you can no longer use. Any unused Spark or Boost balance is lost on deletion and has no cash value.

Just want a break instead?

If you only want to stop being seen for a while, you do not need to delete your account. Incognito browsing hides you from members you have not paged, and you can turn off push notifications at any time in your device settings. Both are reversible; deletion is not.
