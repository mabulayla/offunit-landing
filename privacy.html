Privacy Policy

Version 1.1  ·  Effective June 2026  ·  Applies to the OFFUNIT waitlist and website

Introduction

OFFUNIT ("we," "us," or "our") operates a social networking platform designed for nursing and healthcare communities. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through the OFFUNIT waitlist and website, how we use it, who we share it with, and your rights regarding your data.

By joining the OFFUNIT waitlist you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly:

  • Email address when you join the waitlist
  • City preference (the hub you selected, if provided)
  • Interests you select on the confirmation screen (e.g. Dating, Friends, Networking)

Information collected automatically:

  • General location, browser type, and pages visited via web analytics
  • IP address (used for fraud prevention and analytics)
  • Ad interaction data through Meta Pixel and TikTok Pixel

Information we do not collect at this stage:

  • Payment information of any kind
  • Government-issued ID or sensitive identification
  • Protected Health Information (PHI) or any patient data

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Send you launch announcements and early access invitations for your city
  • Prioritize which city hubs we launch first based on demand
  • Measure the effectiveness of our advertising (anonymized)
  • Prevent fraud and abuse
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not use your information for automated decision-making that has legal or significant effects on you.

3. Email Communications

By joining the waitlist you consent to receive emails from OFFUNIT including launch announcements and early access invitations. Every email includes an unsubscribe link. You can also opt out at any time by emailing contact@offunit.app with the subject "Unsubscribe."

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party for their own marketing purposes. Ever.

Service providers we share data with to operate the waitlist:

  • Supabase — database infrastructure (SOC 2 compliant)
  • Resend — email delivery
  • Meta Pixel — anonymized advertising measurement
  • TikTok Pixel — anonymized advertising measurement

All service providers are contractually required to use your data only to provide services to us.

Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, subpoena, or court order, or to protect the safety of users or the public.

Business transfer: If OFFUNIT is acquired or merges with another company, your data may be transferred. We will notify you before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

5. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in the United States using Supabase, which maintains SOC 2 compliant security practices. We use encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) for all data in transit.

Despite these measures, no system is perfectly secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security and encourage you to use a strong, unique password when you create your account at launch.

6. Data Retention

We retain your waitlist data until you request removal or the app launches and you choose not to create an account. To be removed from the waitlist, email contact@offunit.app and we will delete your data within 30 days.

7. Patient Privacy (HIPAA Notice)

OFFUNIT is not a HIPAA covered entity and does not provide a HIPAA-compliant environment. The platform is a social and networking space — it is not intended for clinical communication or the storage of any patient information.

Users are strictly prohibited from sharing Protected Health Information (PHI) — any information that could identify a patient — on the OFFUNIT platform. We do not knowingly collect PHI. If you believe PHI has been shared, contact us immediately at contact@offunit.app.

8. Your Rights & Choices

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of email communications at any time

To exercise any of these rights, email contact@offunit.app. We will respond within 30 days.

9. California Residents (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Right to know — what personal information we collect and how we use it
  • Right to delete — request deletion of your personal information
  • Right to opt out of sale — we do not sell personal information
  • Right to non-discrimination — we will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights

To exercise your California rights, contact us at contact@offunit.app. We do not sell personal information.

10. Children's Privacy

OFFUNIT is intended for users 18 years of age and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If we learn we have collected information from a minor, we will delete it promptly. If you believe a minor has submitted information, contact us at contact@offunit.app.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the product evolves. When we make material changes, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the waitlist after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact

Questions, concerns, or data requests:

  • Email: contact@offunit.app
  • Website: offunit.app
