Privacy Policy

Version 2.3  ·  Effective August 7, 2026  ·  Applies to the OFFUNIT mobile app, waitlist, and website

Introduction

OFFUNIT ("we," "us," or "our") is a social networking platform built for nurses and healthcare professionals — for dating, friendship, and professional networking. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through the OFFUNIT mobile app, waitlist, and website, how we use it, who we share it with, and the rights and choices you have.

By creating an OFFUNIT account or joining the waitlist, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

Account & profile information you provide:

  • Name, email address, and password (stored as a secure hash — we never see your plain-text password)
  • Age / date of birth (to verify you are 18 or older)
  • Profile photos and an optional featured GIF
  • Bio, icebreaker prompts, and quiz answers (including your personality result)
  • Professional details: nursing specialty and sub-specialty, years of experience, shift type, and certification status
  • Your selected modes (dating, friends, networking) and matching preferences

If you sign in with Google or Apple: we receive your name, email address, and an account identifier from Google or Apple. We never receive your Google or Apple password.

If you verify your nursing license (optional): we collect your license number, license state, and expiration date, and the photo of your license you submit. See Section 4.

Location: with your permission, we collect your device's location coordinates and store them on your profile. We use them to place you in a city hub and to calculate how far away other members are, so we can show you people nearby. Other members are only ever shown your city and an approximate distance — your coordinates are never displayed to them. If you decline the location permission, you can select your city manually instead.

Content you create: chat messages with your matches, posts, comments, and reactions on the OffFeed, badges you earn, and reports you submit.

Your settings and activity on the platform: who you have paged, passed, matched with, or sparked; who you have blocked; whether incognito browsing is turned on; your notification preferences; and your matching filters.

Purchases and paid features: if you subscribe to OFFUNIT+ or buy Sparks or Boosts, we store your subscription status, your remaining Spark and Boost balances, and a record of the purchase. See Section 6.

Collected automatically:

  • Device information, app version, and crash/diagnostic data
  • Push notification tokens (so we can deliver notifications you've enabled)
  • Your IP address, which our servers use to apply rate limits and detect abuse
  • On the website only: general location, browser type, pages visited, IP address, and ad interaction data via Meta Pixel and TikTok Pixel

What we do not collect:

  • Credit card or bank details. All payments are processed entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play, and we never see your payment credentials
  • Protected Health Information (PHI) or any patient data. Sharing it is prohibited (see Section 8)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Operate the platform: create your profile, suggest matches, deliver messages, and run the OffFeed
  • Verify professional credentials for members who choose RN verification
  • Keep the community safe: enforce our guidelines, review reports, and moderate content (see Section 3)
  • Send notifications you've enabled (matches, messages, verification updates) and important account emails
  • Manage subscriptions, track Spark and Boost balances, and unlock paid features
  • Count how many members saw your profile while a Boost was active, so we can show you the result of what you paid for
  • Apply rate limits and detect automated abuse of our servers
  • Fix crashes and improve the app using diagnostic data
  • For the waitlist: send launch announcements and prioritize which city hubs open first
  • Measure the effectiveness of our website advertising (anonymized)
  • Prevent fraud and abuse, and comply with legal obligations

3. Content Moderation & Safety

OFFUNIT uses a combination of automated systems and human review to keep the community safe:

  • Automated screening: text and images you post may be automatically scanned by AI systems to detect patient-privacy violations (PHI) and other content that violates our guidelines. Photo metadata (such as embedded GPS data) is stripped from images you upload.
  • Human review: when content or a profile is reported, our moderation team can review the reported content, the profiles involved, and — for reported conversations — the relevant messages.
  • Enforcement records: if moderation action is taken (warning, content removal, or ban), we keep an internal record of the action and an evidence snapshot of the relevant content. These safety records may be retained even after an account is deleted, for as long as reasonably necessary for the safety of the community, dispute resolution, and legal compliance.

4. Nursing License Verification

Verification is optional and earns a verified badge. When you submit a photo of your nursing license, we use an AI service to extract the license details (number, state, expiration), and a human administrator reviews and confirms the result. Your license photo and details are used solely for verification and are never shown to other members — only the verified badge is visible.

5. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party for their own marketing purposes. Ever.

Other members: your profile (name, age, photos, GIF, bio, prompts, professional details, badges, and verified status) is visible to other OFFUNIT members as part of how the platform works. Your email, precise location, license details, and license photo are never shown to other members.

Some things stay private by design. Other members are never told whether you have an active Boost, how many Sparks or Boosts you have, whether you subscribe to OFFUNIT+, whether you have blocked someone, or that you passed on them. If you browse in incognito mode, members you have not paged do not see you in their deck at all.

Service providers that process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase — database and authentication (SOC 2 compliant)
  • Cloudflare — storage and delivery of profile photos and OffFeed media (Cloudflare R2). Because media is served directly to members' devices, Cloudflare receives the IP address of the device requesting it
  • Railway — backend server hosting
  • Expo — app updates and push notification delivery
  • Anthropic — AI processing for content safety scanning and license-detail extraction
  • Resend — transactional email delivery (password resets, verification decisions)
  • Sentry — crash and error reporting
  • RevenueCat — subscription management (receives purchase receipts from Apple/Google, not your payment details)
  • GIPHY — GIF search. Your search terms, a randomly generated session identifier, and which GIF you select are sent to GIPHY. GIPHY requires this to serve results and measure usage. The identifier is not your OFFUNIT account ID and does not tell GIPHY who you are
  • Google / Apple — sign-in, if you choose social login
  • Meta Pixel / TikTok Pixel — anonymized advertising measurement, website only

All service providers are contractually required to use your data only to provide services to us.

Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, subpoena, or court order, or to protect the safety of users or the public — including reporting to authorities where we are legally obligated to do so.

Business transfer: If OFFUNIT is acquired or merges with another company, your data may be transferred. We will notify you before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

6. Purchases & Paid Features

OFFUNIT offers an optional subscription (OFFUNIT+) and optional one-time purchases (Spark packs and Boost packs). Everything essential to the platform, including profile creation, matching, messaging, the OffFeed, and RN verification, remains free.

How payment works: all purchases are made through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google collects your payment, and we receive only a purchase receipt confirming what you bought. We never receive or store your card number, bank details, or billing address. We use RevenueCat to validate those receipts and tell our servers what you are entitled to.

What we store: your subscription status and renewal date, your remaining Spark and Boost balances, and when a Boost was activated and when it expires.

Boost and the "seen by" counter: when you activate a Boost, your profile is placed near the front of other members' decks for 24 hours, and we count how many times it was shown. That counter is a total number only. We do not tell you who saw you, and we do not tell other members that the profile they are seeing was boosted.

Sparks and Boosts are virtual items with no cash value. If you delete your account, any unused balance is lost. See our Terms of Service for the full purchase and refund terms.

7. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in the United States. If you use OFFUNIT from outside the United States, you understand that your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States, where privacy laws may differ from those in your country. We use encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) for all data in transit, passwords are stored as secure hashes, and access to production data is restricted.

Despite these measures, no system is perfectly secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security and encourage you to use a strong, unique password.

8. Patient Privacy (HIPAA Notice)

OFFUNIT is not a HIPAA covered entity and does not provide a HIPAA-compliant environment. The platform is a social and networking space — it is not intended for clinical communication or the storage of any patient information.

Users are strictly prohibited from sharing Protected Health Information (PHI) — any information that could identify a patient — on the OFFUNIT platform. We use automated scanning to help detect and block PHI before it is posted, but no automated system is perfect. If you believe PHI has been shared, contact us immediately at contact@offunit.app.

9. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. You can permanently delete your account at any time from inside the app (Settings → Delete Account). Deletion removes your profile, photos, matches, messages, posts, and comments from the platform.

Limited data may be retained after deletion where reasonably necessary: safety and moderation records (see Section 3), records we need to comply with legal obligations, and backups that roll off on a fixed schedule.

For step-by-step instructions, what is deleted, and how to request deletion if you can no longer access the app, see Delete Your Account.

Waitlist data is retained until you request removal or you create an account at launch. To be removed from the waitlist, email contact@offunit.app and we will delete your data within 30 days.

10. Your Rights & Choices

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information (most profile data can be edited directly in the app)
  • Delete your account and data (in-app, or by emailing us)
  • Disable push notifications (in your device settings) and opt out of emails at any time
  • Revoke location permission in your device settings (matching will fall back to your selected city)

To exercise any of these rights, email contact@offunit.app. We will respond within 30 days.

11. California Residents (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Right to know — what personal information we collect and how we use it
  • Right to delete — request deletion of your personal information
  • Right to opt out of sale — we do not sell personal information
  • Right to non-discrimination — we will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights

To exercise your California rights, contact us at contact@offunit.app. We do not sell personal information.

12. European Economic Area & United Kingdom (GDPR)

If you are in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland, you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation and equivalent UK law. Moe & Bros Enterprises, LLC is the data controller for the information described in this policy.

Our legal bases for processing:

  • Performance of a contract — creating your account, matching, messaging, running the OffFeed, and fulfilling purchases you make
  • Consent — location access, push notifications, and submitting a nursing license for verification. You can withdraw consent at any time in your device or app settings
  • Legitimate interests — keeping the community safe, moderating content, preventing fraud and abuse, and fixing crashes
  • Legal obligation — responding to lawful requests and retaining records we are required to keep

Sensitive data: depending on how you use the platform, your profile may reveal information some laws treat as sensitive, such as your sexual orientation (through the modes you select) or your health-related profession. You provide this information voluntarily and, by choosing to publish it on your profile, you consent to it being shown to other members. You can edit or remove it at any time.

Your rights: access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, objection to processing, and data portability. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

International transfers: our servers are in the United States. Where we transfer personal data out of the EEA or UK, we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (and the UK Addendum) with our service providers.

To exercise any GDPR right, email contact@offunit.app. We will respond within one month.

13. Age Requirement

OFFUNIT is intended for adults 18 years of age and older, and the app is rated 17+ in the app stores. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If we learn we have collected information from a minor, we will delete it promptly. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us at contact@offunit.app.
