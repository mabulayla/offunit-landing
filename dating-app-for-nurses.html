A dating app for nurses who work 12s, nights, and every other weekend

Most dating apps assume you're free Tuesday at 7. If you just worked three nights in a row, you're asleep Tuesday at 7. OFFUNIT is a free dating app built around the nursing schedule instead.

Why dating is harder on a nurse's schedule

It isn't that nurses are bad at dating. The schedule works against you in ways other people don't see:

  • Your days off move. A Wednesday off is great until every match wants to meet on Saturday, which you're working.
  • Nights flip your whole week. After a stretch of nights you're sleeping through the afternoon most people use to text, plan, and meet up.
  • A 12 leaves nothing in the tank. "Want to grab a drink after work?" lands differently at 7:45pm after 13 hours on your feet.
  • Plans get cancelled for you. Mandatory overtime, a call-in, or a shift that runs long. People outside healthcare tend to read that as flaky.
  • Work talk needs a filter. The dark humor that gets you through a shift doesn't always land on a first date with an accountant.

None of that means you can't date someone outside healthcare. It just means the first few weeks go a lot smoother with someone who already gets it, or at least expects it.

What to look for in a dating app as a nurse

People who understand shift work

The biggest time saver is skipping the "wait, you work nights?" conversation. On an app where everyone is in or around healthcare, a 0700 coffee date after a night shift is normal, not weird.

Schedule out in the open

If you can see someone works nights or rotates before you match, you can tell right away whether your schedules will overlap at all.

A way to know people are who they say

Anyone can write "RN" in a bio. License verification means the badge actually means something.

Room for more than dating

Sometimes what you need this month is a gym buddy who also works nights, not a relationship. An app that lets you switch between dating and friends means you don't have to keep three apps going.

How OFFUNIT works

OFFUNIT is one app with four parts, all on the same profile:

  • Dating. A swipe deck of nurses and healthcare people near you. Profiles show your shift type, so night shifters can find each other.
  • Friends. Same idea, no romance. Built for new grads, people who just moved, and travel nurses starting a new contract.
  • Networking. Show your certs and specialty and find mentors or people in the unit you're trying to get into.
  • OffFeed. A feed for nurse memes, wins and real talk. Post under your name or anonymously. No patients, no managers.

Verification is optional. RNs can verify their license for free and get a verified badge. Nursing students and CNAs are welcome too, and their profiles say so, so you always know who you're talking to.

It's free. Swiping, matching and messaging cost nothing. There are optional paid extras if you want them.

Dating tips for night shift nurses

  • Lead with your schedule. Say early that you work nights or rotate. Anyone who's put off by it was going to be put off in week three anyway.
  • Make mornings a date slot. Breakfast or coffee right after a night shift, before you crash, is a real option. So is a late lunch on your first day off.
  • Protect your sleep anyway. Don't schedule a first date in the middle of your sleep window just to be accommodating. Tired you is not the version you want them to meet.
  • Send your schedule once it's out. Sharing your next few weeks of shifts takes the guesswork out of planning and shows you're actually interested.
  • Have one non-work story ready. Shift stories are great with other nurses. With someone new, having something else to talk about helps.
